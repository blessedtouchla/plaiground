How to get your music on Spotify.

You cannot upload straight to Spotify. Here is the path independent artists use, step by step.

Before you upload

Have these ready

  • A finished, mixed and mastered WAV file.
  • Square cover art, 3000 x 3000 pixels is the safe size.
  • Song title, artist name and every writer’s name.
  • A signed split sheet if more than one person wrote it.
  • An honest label: Human, AI-assisted or Fully AI.

Step by step

Release it

  • Pick a distributor. On plai it is the Release flow.
  • Upload the song and cover, add credits and splits.
  • Choose stores and a release date a few weeks out.
  • Claim your Spotify for Artists profile once the release is live or scheduled.
  • Pitch the unreleased song to Spotify editors from Spotify for Artists.

Questions

Quick answers

Can I upload music to Spotify myself? Not directly. Spotify takes new music from distributors, so you release through a distribution service like plai.

How long does it take? Plan on a few weeks before your release date. That leaves time for store review and for pitching your song to Spotify editors from Spotify for Artists.

Can AI-assisted songs go on Spotify? Yes, if you own the rights and label it honestly. Stores are tightening rules on fully AI and spam uploads, so clear credits matter.
