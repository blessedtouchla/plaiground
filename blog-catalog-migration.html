Catalog migration without starting over.

Sep 18, 2026. The account is free. Bring the songs you already released.

Bring the catalog

The songs already exist. The business should not start at zero.

You move an existing catalog onto PLAIGROUND. You do not pretend the last five years never happened.

Who gets it

Every artist. The free account has no monthly charge.

What moves

The recordings you already released elsewhere. The point is to keep the catalog on one account instead of a second pile of logins.

What does not change

Tell us what a human did. Everyone signs the split sheet. No cloning existing artists. Artists keep 100% of their royalties.

How to think about it

  • There is one free account. You do not buy a higher tier to move a catalog you already have.
  • Migration is not a magic rewrite of store history. Stores still have their own files. The job is to point the next chapter at one dashboard.
  • Publishing is not distribution. If the compositions were never registered, moving the masters does not fix that. Every artist can register both rooms.
  • Fully AI still delivers even if a store will not pay. A migrated Fully AI track does not suddenly become payable.
  • Video Collect is opt-in per song. Fully AI cannot opt in. Covers and samples get a review. Migration does not skip that.
  • You do not pitch all day after the move. We curate and email. You push a button.

If the catalog is the thing, bring it. If you have one song, start there. The account is the same.

For artists who’d rather be making the next one than reading a distribution agreement.
