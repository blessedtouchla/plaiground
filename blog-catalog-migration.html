WANNA PLAI?

Catalog migration without starting over.

Sep 18, 2026. Pro unlocks it. Creator does not.

Bring the catalog

The songs already exist. The business should not start at zero.

Pro is Creator, unlimited, plus catalog migration. You move an existing catalog onto PLAIGROUND. You do not pretend the last five years never happened.

Who gets it

Pro only. $19.99 a month or $199 a year. Unlimited distribution. Unlimited publishing registrations. Catalog migration.

What moves

The recordings you already released elsewhere. The point is to keep the catalog in one membership instead of a second pile of logins.

What does not change

Tell us what a human did. Everyone signs the split sheet. No cloning existing artists. 0% royalty cut. Membership is the fee.

How to think about it

  • Basic is one song for the life of the account. Creator caps at 8 distribution and 8 publishing a month. Pro takes the cap off and adds migration.
  • Migration is not a magic rewrite of store history. Stores still have their own files. The job is to point the next chapter at one dashboard.
  • Publishing is not distribution. If the compositions were never registered, moving the masters does not fix that. Creator and Pro register both rooms.
  • Fully AI still delivers even if a store will not pay. A migrated Fully AI track does not suddenly become payable.
  • Video Collect is opt-in per song. Fully AI cannot opt in. Covers and samples get a review. Migration does not skip that.
  • You do not pitch all day after the move. We curate and email. You push a button.

If the catalog is the thing, Pro is the plan. If you have one song, start Basic.
