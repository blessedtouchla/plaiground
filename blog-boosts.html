The trap

Most “promotion” is just buying bots.

Fake streams. Fake followers. Fake engagement. Stores detect it. Charts ignore it. You waste money and risk the account. That is not marketing. That is noise.

What we refuse

Bot farms. Purchased plays. Inflated numbers that never convert to real fans or real royalties.

What we build

Chart Push. Streaming Push. Social Push. Video Collect. Targeted. Accountable. Tied to your release.

Who gets them

Every artist. None of them are for sale on the public site.

The four Boosts

  • Chart Push. Broad curator and playlist campaign built for chart-eligible activity. Real outreach. Not manufactured streams.
  • Streaming Push. Playlist pitching on Spotify. Outreach aimed at audiences most likely to keep the track. Save rate matters more than raw plays.
  • Social Push. Real people sharing your song on Instagram and short-form video. Not bots. Not purchased views.
  • Video Collect. Get paid when someone uses your song in a video. YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok. We take 0%. You get 100% of the payout. Fully AI tracks cannot opt in.

Boosts are open to every artist. They are not a separate product you can buy on the open site.

Every signed-in artist can add one to a release when a package is offered. The price is shown before you pay.

PLAIGROUND takes none of the royalties. Artists keep 100% of their royalties. Stores still take their cut. We do not add another one.
