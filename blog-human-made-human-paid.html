Human made. Human paid. Musicians want consent, credit and pay for AI.

Oct 22, 2026. The biggest US musicians union just drew a line.

The musicians union just got loud.

On Oct 20, the American Federation of Musicians launched a campaign called Human Made, Human Paid.

Three words carry it. Consent. Credit. Compensation.

What they are asking for

Consent: musicians get to say yes or no before their work trains AI.

Credit: clear, honest credit for the people who played.

Compensation: pay when their work makes money in AI systems or licensing deals.

The union says it is not here to fight technology or tools for making music. It is fighting for the people who make it.

Why now

The union says it goes back to the table on Nov 3. The contract is the Sound Recording Labor Agreement, with UMG, Sony, Warner and Hollywood Records.

The union also says it sued Universal, Warner Records and Atlantic Records over licensing recording catalogs to AI platforms without paying musicians.

That case is the union's claim. A court has not ruled on it.
