What stays human

  • Taste stays human. What we ship, what we refuse, what we boost. A model does not pick the bright line.
  • Rights stay human. Voice cloning of an existing artist is not allowed. Inaccurate disclosure is not allowed. Someone has to attest.
  • Money stays human. PLAIGROUND takes none of the royalties. Membership is the fee. Payouts do not go to a nameless bot.
  • PLAI walks the release. She does not replace the artist. She does not pitch all day for you so you can spam stores. We curate and email. You push a button.
  • SIVA is the example, not a loophole. Name. Face. Split sheet. Tell us what a human did. Official charts gate wholly AI tracks. SIQA is the parallel AI-native chart.
  • Traditional. AI-assisted. Fully AI. We still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay.
