Traditional distribution. Not an AI dump.

PLAIGROUND accepts traditional, AI-assisted, and fully AI. That does not mean every upload gets the AI stamp. If a human wrote it, played it, and sang it, you check No AI. We send No AI. Spotify, Apple, Amazon, the rest — they see a human recording. The way DistroKid or TuneCore would send a human recording. We do not relabel your work to ride an AI wave.

No AI

A human made the recording. Normal store pay rules. Official charts can count it if the board’s other rules hold. Video Collect can opt in. No Made With AI. No AI Persona.

AI-assisted

A human work with AI help. Different box. We still distribute. Monetize follows each store. Not the same as Fully AI. Not the same as No AI.

Fully AI

We still deliver. Some stores will not pay. Tidal may list and not pay. Deezer tags it. Official boards gate wholly AI-generated recordings. SIQA is the other chart. We do not invent a pay row.

What we will not do

  • We will not stamp a 100% human track as AI so it looks like a trend.
  • We will not drop your vocal into an AI Persona profile because the platform is loud about bots.
  • We will not hide AI either. If a model sang the centre, you mark Fully AI. If a model helped, you mark AI-assisted.
  • We will not clone a living artist. Stolen voices do not ship.
  • We will not rewrite your disclosure after you sign the split sheet.
