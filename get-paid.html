Set up the accounts that help you collect

Releasing a song is step one. These free or low cost signups help make sure money from radio, streaming and your songwriting can find you.

Start here

The guides

  • Register with SoundExchange as a featured artist: About 20 to 30 minutes, plus a few days for approval.
  • Register with SoundExchange as a rights owner: About 30 minutes, plus a few days for approval.
  • Sign up with The MLC: About 20 minutes to sign up, then time to add each song.
  • Join BMI as a songwriter: About 15 to 20 minutes. Your IPI number may take a few days.
  • Get your own ISRC codes: About 15 minutes to apply. Codes usually arrive within a few days.

Order

What to do first

  • Join BMI as a songwriter and get your IPI.
  • Register with SoundExchange as a featured artist.
  • If you own your masters, register as a SoundExchange rights owner too.
  • Sign up with The MLC and add your songs.
  • Get your own ISRC prefix only if you need it.

Have ready

Keep these close

A government photo ID and your phone for ID checks, your date of birth, your Social Security number or EIN, a signed W-9 if you register a company, and your IPI once BMI sends it. plai can prefill your song list and walk you through each step, but ID checks and signatures are yours to do.
