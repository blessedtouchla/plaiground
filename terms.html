3. What PLAIGROUND is

PLAIGROUND helps creators release and package music — human, AI-assisted, or fully AI — with honest labeling.

Depending on what you buy and what you opt into, we may:

  1. Host a free account and DIY after-upload tools (disclosure, profile/persona, EPK template, payout setup).
  2. Deliver master recordings to digital stores and platforms (distribution).
  3. Offer optional A&R Act Pack packaging (persona kit, hosted EPK build, related act tools as described at purchase).
  4. Offer optional Sync packaging (materials only; no placement promise).
  5. Generate split sheets and collect electronic signatures.
  6. Act as publisher of compositions you opt into publishing for, under a separate Publishing Agreement.
  7. Offer optional Marketing Boost or similar promotion only when a live package is actually sold (if nothing is for sale yet, these Terms do not create a right to buy one).

Distribution (masters / streaming revenue) and publishing (composition / songwriter royalties) are separate. Paying for one does not include the other.

Sister products (for example PLAInow listening, SIQA Charts listen pages, PLAI coach) may have their own rules. If they conflict with these Terms for a PLAIGROUND account action, these Terms control for distribution, publishing, packs, and payouts.

9. AI labeling and AI tool rules (current)

9.1 Classes we accept

You may release tracks labeled Human, AI-assisted, or Fully AI. You choose the class on upload. We pass your attestation to stores. We do not invent your class by scanning audio and overwriting your attestation.

Stores set their own listing, monetization, and playlist rules. We do not promise a listing, payout rate, or playlist for any class.

9.2 Commercial AI tool license

If any AI tool (including Suno, Udio, or similar) was used, you warrant that at generation time you used a paid commercial plan that allows commercial distribution and monetization. Free / non-commercial plans do not qualify. Upgrading later does not retroactively license earlier output.

9.3 Prohibited

  • Concealing AI involvement when the track is AI-assisted or Fully AI
  • Mass spam / algorithm-gaming uploads
  • Infringing content

9.4 Proof and refusal

We may request proof of AI-tool subscription, licenses, or other rights docs at any time. We may refuse, hold, or remove a release if proof is missing or disclosure looks false.

If you opt in and we accept the work:

  • Writers need active PRO affiliation for full registration where required.

12. Payouts, clawbacks, fraud

Payouts happen only after stores report and we actually receive allocable funds, and after you set a valid payout method and any required tax forms (W-9 / W-8BEN or equivalent when we require them).

Balances may show $0 until a release is live and reported.

If a store, partner, or bank claws back amounts, fines us, or charges fraud/penalty costs because of your content or activity, those amounts may be deducted from your balance or invoiced to you.

Artificial streaming, bot promotion, or pay-for-play that violates store rules is forbidden. We may freeze royalties on a disputed title until the dispute is resolved.
