150,000 songs a day. The pile is not the product.

Sep 18, 2026. Luminate’s CEO put the new number on the record. The old 100,000-a-day line is dead.

The flood

You are not competing with a song. You are competing with a hose.

Nearly 150,000 tracks a day now land on streaming services. Deezer has said fully AI can be more than half of new uploads at peak. Apple has said more than a third of monthly uploads look 100% AI. Suno-scale generators mint millions of files that never even hit a store. Volume is free. Attention is not.

The number

Luminate, mid-September 2026: close to 150,000 tracks uploaded every day. That is the new floor. Catalog already eats most listening. New music has to earn a seat.

The mix

Fully AI is a large slice of the hose. That does not make Fully AI illegal to distribute. It makes silence expensive. Stores and charts now ask who made the centre of the recording.

The job
