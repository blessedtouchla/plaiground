Questions

Quick answers

Can I get my music on Spotify and Apple Music with PLAIGROUND? Yes. You upload your song and cover, fill in credits and splits, and we deliver your release to the stores you pick, including Spotify and Apple Music.

Does PLAIGROUND take a cut of my royalties? No. PLAIGROUND takes none of your recording royalties. Store payouts depend on your streams and each store's own rates, so no one can promise a set amount.

Can I release AI-assisted music? Yes, if you label it honestly. Every release is tagged Human, AI-assisted or Fully AI, and you tell us what a human did on the track.

Is it free to start? The account is free and new accounts get free starter credits to try every tool. Distribution needs a plan, and the price is shown before you pay.
