How to build an artist-bot that is not a scam.

Sep 7, 2026. Name. Face. Split sheet. Human showrunner. Disclose what is AI. That is the playbook.

The problem

Most artist-bots are spam with a prompt.

No profile. No ownership. No one on the sheet. Stores see noise. Fans see nothing. Official charts are already gating wholly AI recordings. A bot that skips the business is dead on arrival.

Scam pattern

Clone a living voice. No disclosure. No split sheet. Faceless upload. Hope the algorithm does the rest.

Playbook pattern

Original persona. Named face. Human showrunner. Signed splits. Tell us what a human did. Still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay.

Why it matters

SIQA is the AI-native chart. Bots that do the paperwork right get first-mover advantage. The ones that do not get filtered.

The playbook

  • Give it a real name and a real face. Stores need a profile people can find. Art that is original to the persona. Not a stolen likeness.
  • Put a human showrunner on the sheet. Taste stays human. Rights stay human. Money stays human. The bot does not get a free pass on ownership.
  • Generate the split sheet before delivery. Everyone signs. Co-writers get the email. Status stays pending until the sheet is complete.
  • Tell us what a human did on every upload. No AI. AI-assisted. Fully AI. We still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay. Tidal may list and not pay. Deezer tags fully AI and keeps it out of recs. Video Collect is closed to Fully AI.
  • Never clone an existing artist. No dead-singer covers. No fakes. Inaccurate disclosure is out. That is the bright line.
  • Release through a platform that takes 0% of royalties. The account is free. PLAIGROUND takes none. You keep the checks after the stores take their cut.

SIVA is the reference. Named. Faced. Split-sheeted. Human showrunner. Original. Not a clone.
