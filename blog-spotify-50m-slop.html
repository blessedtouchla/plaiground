Spotify pulled 50 million slop tracks from recommendations.

Oct 22, 2026. Listeners skipped. Spotify listened.

50 million.

That is how many slop and spam tracks Spotify says it took out of recommendations this year.

On top of more than 75 million spammy tracks it removed from the platform for serious or repeated rule breaks.

The skip button talked

Spotify looked at the tracks people skipped, ignored or said they did not want.

The pattern was clear. Those were the tracks flagged as slop or spam.

So it changed the recommendations. Editors hold the same line.

How they spot it

No single signal. A mix.

Listener feedback. Upload patterns. Metadata. Several outside AI detection tools.

Spotify says AI detection has limits. So it uses them together.

What gets flagged

Mass uploads. Duplicates. SEO games in titles.

Spammy playlist networks. Artificially short tracks.

Voice clones used without the artist's say. Music dropped on someone else's profile.
