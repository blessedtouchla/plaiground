How the money lands.

Sep 19, 2026. Stores pay first. We do not print cash.

The pipe

Totals stay at $0 until a release is live and a store reports.

PLAIGROUND takes no commission. 0% cut of royalties. Membership is the only PLAIGROUND fee. Stores take their usual cut. Money moves after they pay.

Basic

Free. One song for life. You can see totals. You cannot withdraw. Upgrade to Creator or Pro to retrieve.

Creator and Pro

Retrieve payouts. Publishing. Boosts. Analytics. Creator has a monthly cap. Pro takes the cap off.

The rule

Payouts only after real royalties arrive. Not before stores pay. No live release, no reported royalties.

What that means in practice

  • Upload. Sign splits. Push the button. The song hits 150 platforms. Then you wait on the store cycle, not on us inventing a number.
  • Video Collect pays when someone uses the song in a video. You get 100% of that payout. Fully AI cannot opt in.
  • Publishing is a different check. Societies, not stores. Register after the first release is live. Not on Basic.
  • A store can list a song and still not pay. Tidal may list wholly AI and not pay. We do not invent a pay row.
  • If you need the money moving, leave Basic. Free gets the first song out. Paid gets retrieve.
  • You do not chase three dashboards. One membership. One pipe. 0% on the way through.

The song is finished. The business is not your job. The payout is a report, not a vibe.
