Research portal

Five areas for an artist or a song: presence, credits, analytics, opportunities, and payments. The EPK stays the curated public page. This portal does not certify ownership, clearance, or earnings.

A name is only a starting point. Confirm the Wikidata item, or start from a MusicBrainz ID, a store artist link, an ISRC, or an ISWC. One match is still a candidate until you confirm it.

Artist presence

Live results come from Wikidata (CC0), checked at the time on each field. MusicBrainz pages are links you open. Found so far means this query, not the whole internet. Profiles and follower counts are not invented here.

Open these yourself

Song and credits

Recordings (ISRC) and compositions (ISWC) stay separate. A public credit or a Wikidata identifier is not the chain of title. A registry miss is not the same as unregistered.

Catalog progress

Three separate counts, including songs released through other distributors. They are not added into one score, and they are not a legal clearance.

Sign in to keep this list on this browser for your account. It is not uploaded, and it is not visible to another account on this browser.

Add a row you already know

One song a line. Do not paste royalty statements, bank details, or payment reports. A code you type is not treated as a registry match.

Analytics

Not available in this free portal. There is no Spotify analytics, no Chartmetric, and no Soundcharts. Standard Spotify access is not cleared for this use, and those audience tools are paid services we have not connected.

Your PLAIGROUND account analytics stay on the Analytics page. They are your account figures, not a public estimate and not a number this research page invents.

Open account analytics

AudD, ACRCloud, Muso, and Chartmetric are not connected. This page does not ask you to buy them.

Opportunities

Not built. There is no sync brief list, no festival list, and nothing is submitted for you. A later version would show eligibility, deadlines, and fees before anyone applied. This one does not.

Payments and royalties

This portal does not estimate royalties and does not take statement uploads. Exact money stays in the account that was paid: your distributor, society, or MLC statements, and the Earnings and Payouts pages for this PLAIGROUND account.

A public credit is not a payment. A missing registry row is not proof that money is owed or that a song is unregistered.

What is connected, and what is not

  • Working lookup: Wikidata search and identifiers (CC0), from your request, rate limited.
  • Link only: MusicBrainz, ASCAP repertory, BMI repertoire, Songview, The MLC, US Copyright Office, USPTO, ISRC search. You read those sites. We do not copy them.
  • Not connected: MusicBrainz web service (their docs say commercial use of that hosted service needs their commercial plans), Apple catalog, Deezer catalog, SecondHandSongs, Spotify analytics, Suno, audio fingerprinting, SIQA registry, AudD, ACRCloud, Muso, Chartmetric, Soundcharts.

MusicBrainz core data is CC0, and supplementary data such as tags, ratings, and annotations is not requested. No MusicBrainz database dump is downloaded. SIQA, if it were connected later, would be an attestation or editorial record, not an audio detector. A missing SIQA row would mean unknown, not human and not AI.

The EPK is separate. Open the EPK when you want a public presentation. Publishing registration is a different page and is not submitted from here.
