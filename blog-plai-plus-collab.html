The room

PLAI+ is where you find each other.

Singers look for beats. Beat-makers look for a voice. That meeting lives on plainow.com. Not a pile of full files. A short preview. An invite. Two yeses. Then you share what you agreed to share.

Invite

Unlock

How the trust works

  • Write the credits and the splits before the full stems move. Honesty first. Trust is the product.
  • Do not wait for a stamp that is not on the page yet. Names. Percents. Agree in the room.
  • PLAIGROUND takes none of the royalties. Membership is the fee. You keep the masters.
  • When you release, disclose the AI. No AI. AI-assisted. Fully AI. The stamp is yours.
  • A generator will not hunt down a stolen copy for you. The lock is the yes. The preview is short on purpose.
  • This is not a flyer that promises a royalty fortune. It is a room. Find the person. Make the song.
