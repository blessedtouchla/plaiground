Sep 18, 2026. Article 50 is already law. The grace period is only for old generators, and only for machine-readable marks.

The law

Providers mark the output. You still disclose the release.

Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026. Providers of systems that generate synthetic audio must mark that output so a machine can detect it. Systems already on the market before 2 August get until 2 December 2026 for that mark. Deployers must disclose deepfakes. A cloned living voice is the problem case. Art can keep a lighter credit so the work still plays. The grace period is not a license to lie on a split sheet.

What started Aug 2

Transparency duties. Chatbots say they are bots. New generators mark synthetic audio, image, video, text. Deepfakes get a human-readable disclose.

What waits until Dec 2

Only the machine-readable mark for generators that were already selling before August 2. New systems do not get that extra window.

What maps to music

IFPI and RIAA already proposed two track badges in July: AI-Generated and AI-Assisted. Lead vocal or key instrumental generated by AI sits in Generated. Humans on the lead vocal and primary instruments can sit in Assisted.
