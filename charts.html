How it’s calculated

These are SIQA’s weekly charts (week of Sept 15, 2026 until they update).

SIQA’s public score is 50% streams and airplay + 50% social impact. Listening: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube. Social: TikTok, Instagram Reels. Discovery includes Shazam. Inner math is theirs and proprietary. Updates Tuesdays 12pm PT. No pay-to-chart.

We love this because it is an honest AI chart with a disclosed method, not a fake playlist buy.

If you run charts (genre, language, country, AI-class) and want them listened-to on PLAIGROUND, write emailplaiground@gmail.com. Official SIQA stays https://www.thesiqa.com/charts and https://www.thesiqa.com/charts-faq.
