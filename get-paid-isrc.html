Get paid

Get your own ISRC codes

An ISRC is the ID code for one recording. It is how plays get matched to you.

Time: About 15 minutes to apply. Codes usually arrive within a few days.

Have ready

What you need

  • Proof you own the recording, or your company name
  • An email and a payment card for the $95 fee

Steps

How to do it

  1. Most artists do not need this. plai can assign an ISRC to each new recording for you.
  2. If you own your masters and want your own codes, apply for a rights owner prefix at usisrc.org.
  3. Pay the one-time $95 fee. The prefix is yours to keep.
  4. Give each new recording its own code. Never reuse a code, and never give a new code to a song that already has one.
  5. Use the same code everywhere: your release, SoundExchange and your song list.

Good to know

What this does

Songs that are already out keep the codes they have. A clean ISRC helps you collect, because digital radio reports plays by ISRC. No signup guarantees income. You may earn when your music is played and reported.

Official source: usisrc.org
