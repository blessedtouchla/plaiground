The rules, short

  • EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties applied from August 2, 2026. Providers mark AI output so it can be detected. Deployers label certain deepfakes. Cloning a real artist’s voice is the problem case.
  • Apple Music asked providers to tag AI use and is rolling visible Made With AI labels by the end of 2026. Spotify added AI credits and an AI Persona label. Music from AI Persona profiles stays out of personalized recs.
  • Deezer tags fully AI, keeps it out of recs, and does not pay streams they call fraudulent. Tidal may list wholly AI and not pay. Luminate said it will add AI Generated labels for customers later this year.
  • Industry groups proposed two stamps: AI-generated and AI-assisted. That maps to our three boxes. No AI. AI-assisted. Fully AI.
  • FAQ is current as of August 28, 2026. Platforms change this. We send the file. They may label it. We do not promise they will pay every stream.
  • Video Collect, YouTube Content ID, Meta Rights, TikTok video-claim: Fully AI cannot opt in. No AI and AI-assisted can. AI-assisted may get a review warning.
