    AI and authorship

    Yes. Traditional, AI-assisted, and fully AI tracks are accepted. You declare what is human and what is AI-assisted on upload.

    Monetize (AI)

    Full AI tracks are allowed. We still distribute even when a store will not pay. We still send the release.

    We still send the release. We only list what is confirmed. We do not invent a pay or no-pay row for every store.

    Tidal: will not pay royalties on music they identify as wholly AI-generated. They may still list it.

    Deezer: tags fully AI, keeps it out of recs/playlists, and does not pay streams they call fraudulent.

    Video Collect / YouTube Content ID / Meta Rights / TikTok video-claim: Fully AI cannot opt in. No AI and AI-assisted can. AI-assisted may get a review warning.

    Spotify, Apple, YouTube Music, and most others: we send it. They may label it. They fight spam and impersonation. We do not promise they will pay every stream.

    AI-assisted is treated as a human work with AI help. We distribute. Monetize follows each store’s normal rules plus their spam/impersonation rules. Not the same as Fully AI.

    No AI follows normal store pay rules.

    Splits and publishing

    Publishing is composition money collected by societies, not stores. It is separate from store streams. Register after your first release is live. Publishing is included on Creator and Pro. It is not on Basic. See How you get paid.

    We generate the split sheet. Everyone signs before delivery. Writer 1 signs in the page. Co-writers get an email.

    No. Only the person submitting pays the release fees. There is no admin fee on the split sheet.

    Publishing registration is on Creator and Pro. It is separate from distribution and is not included on Basic. There is no publishing fee from PLAIGROUND beyond membership. See How you get paid.

    Money and plans

    PLAIGROUND takes no commission. 0% cut of royalties. The only PLAIGROUND fee is membership. See How you get paid.

    Money shows as $0 until a release is actually live and stores report. Payouts only after real royalties arrive. Payouts are quarterly, with a $10 minimum. Basic can see totals but cannot withdraw. Creator and Pro can retrieve payouts. See How you get paid.

    Three plans. Basic is $0 forever: one song for life, distribution to the live store catalog, split sheets, and no publishing, Boost, or withdraw. Creator is Basic with the paid features unlocked: publishing, Marketing Boost, album, advanced analytics, and retrieve / get paid. Then the cap: 8 distribution uploads a month and 8 publishing registrations a month, counted separately. Creator is $14.99 a month. Yearly is $149. Pro unlocks unlimited: $19.99 a month or $199 a year. Pro includes catalog migration. Creator does not. Marketing Boost packages are not for sale yet.

    Boosts are marketing campaigns, not a membership plan. Chart Push, Streaming Push, Social Push, and Video Collect live on the Boosts page. Creator and Pro unlock that page. Packages are not for sale yet.

    MSP means Multiple Streams of Revenue. After a song is published and live, MSP is extra ways that song can earn: publishing plus distribution streams, and later marketing. The MSP section is only for published, live songs.

    Take a song down from the release page with Remove. A live store release asks stores to take it down and stays listed until they confirm. Draft, pending, and processing releases are removed from PLAIGROUND after you confirm. Plan cancellation is handled from the account page.
