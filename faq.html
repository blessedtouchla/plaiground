Money and plans

Plan fees and per-work fees are listed on Plans and Pricing and in the Terms. There is no extra commission line on the writer split sheet.

Royalties appear in your dashboard automatically. Payouts run monthly once your balance clears the minimum.

Three plans. Basic is $0 forever: one song for life, distribution to 150 platforms, split sheets, and no publishing, Boost, or withdraw. Upgrade to Creator or Pro. Creator and Pro are the same product. Creator is $14.99 a month or $149 a year: 8 distribution uploads a month and 8 publishing registrations a month, counted separately, plus publishing, Marketing Boost, album, advanced analytics, and retrieve / get paid. Pro is $19.99 a month or $199 a year: the same features, unlimited on both caps. Marketing Boost packages are not for sale yet.

You can take a song down from Settings after it is live. Plan cancellation is handled from the account page.
