Song royalties explained simply

The two sides of every song, the main royalty types, and who collects each one.

Part 1

Two copyrights in every song

The recording (the master) and the composition (the words and melody). Each one can earn its own money.

Part 2

The main royalty types

  • Recording royalties from streams and downloads, paid through your distributor.
  • Performance royalties for the composition, collected by your PRO.
  • Mechanical royalties for the composition, collected in the US by the MLC.
  • Sync fees when a song is licensed for video.

Part 3

An honest note

Pay per stream changes by store, country and listener plan, so no one can promise a fixed rate. plai takes none of your recording royalties.
