Editorial policy

Nov 3, 2026. Who writes this blog, what we disclose, and how to tell us we got something wrong.

By Victoria Imtanes, Editor

This is the editorial policy for the PLAIGROUND blog. It is a page about how we publish, not a terms of service and not legal advice.

We are independent

PLAIGROUND writes this blog on its own. We are not a desk inside another label, distributor, or newsroom. Opinion pieces carry a byline. Reported pieces link the sources the facts came from. If we cannot verify a name, a number, a quote, or a date from a source we can point to, we leave it out.

We are also a music release platform

Disclosure, said plainly: PLAIGROUND is not only a blog. PLAIGROUND is a music release platform. We distribute music. We ask artists to label that music. When a story mentions our own product, you are reading the company that sells the product, not an outside reviewer. The About page says who founded the company. Treat product mentions as coming from us.

How we label music

We use three labels, and we use them the same way on the blog and on upload.

  • Human: people wrote and performed it.
  • AI-assisted: a human made the key creative choices and AI helped.
  • Fully AI: generated with little human authorship.

Honest means the label matches what a person actually did. We do not upgrade a generated track to Human to make it easier to pitch, and we do not stamp Human work as AI. The longer explanation is on AI music labels and in the plain guide to credits.

Corrections

If a story has a factual error, we correct the page. We add a note at the bottom of the post saying what changed and the date of the change. The original mistake should not stay up as if we never wrote it. Send corrections, and the source that shows the error, to emailplaiground@gmail.com.

How to reach us

General questions, artist questions, and corrections: emailplaiground@gmail.com.

Press and media: press@wannaplai.com.

The contact page lists both. Please use the press address for interviews, photos, and coverage requests, and the general address for everything else.
