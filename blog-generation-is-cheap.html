Sep 12, 2026. Two rooms. Only one of them pays.

Two rooms

The file is easy. The company around the file is not.

A model will give you a track in a minute. A store will not give you a career because you pressed generate. Official charts are gating wholly AI-generated tracks. SIQA is the parallel AI-native chart. The people who win are the ones who did the business.

Room one

Make the sound. Traditional. AI-assisted. Fully AI. Taste still decides. A clone of a living act is not taste. It is a reject.

Room two

Name. Face. Split sheet. Attestation. Delivery. Publishing. Payouts. Boosts that are not bot farms.

The moat

Room two. Generation gets cheaper every month. Paperwork does not. That is why we built Plai.

What we refuse to romanticize

  • Daily pitching is not a job you should have. We curate. We email. You push a button.
  • A faceless spam account is not an artist. SIVA has a name, a face, and a split sheet. So should you.
  • Fake streams do not count. Chart Push, Streaming Push, Social Push, Video Collect. Real people. Real campaigns.
  • We still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay. Honesty is part of the moat. A hidden prompt is not.
  • Bots and automation get first-mover advantage when the packet is clean. The model is not the advantage. The release is.

The account is free. There is no monthly charge on the free account and no upload cap tied to a tier. Artists keep 100% of their royalties.

PLAIGROUND takes none of the royalties. The account is free.
