How you get paid.

PLAIGROUND takes no commission. 0% cut of royalties.

Royalties

PLAIGROUND takes none.

The only PLAIGROUND fee is membership. There is no $199 per work, no $49 per release, and no publishing or distribution fee from PLAIGROUND.

0% commission

PLAIGROUND takes no commission. 0% cut of royalties.

Membership is the fee

Basic is free, 1 song. Creator is $14.99/mo or $149/yr. Pro is $19.99/mo or $199/yr.

Stores take their cut

Stores (Spotify, Apple, and the rest) take their own usual cut. Royalties pass through after the stores pay. PLAIGROUND takes none.

Plans

  • Creator is Basic with the paid features unlocked. Then a monthly cap. Pro unlocks unlimited and catalog migration.
  • Creator: 8 distribution uploads and 8 publishing registrations a month.
  • Pro: unlimited on both caps, plus catalog migration.
  • Both get publishing, Marketing Boost, album, advanced analytics, and payouts / withdraw.
  • Publishing registration is separate from distribution. Publishing is Creator and Pro. It is not included on Basic.

Money starts at $0

Money shows as $0 until a release is actually live and stores report.

  • No live release, no reported royalties
  • Totals stay at $0 until stores send numbers
02

Payouts after royalties arrive

Payouts only after real royalties arrive. Not before stores pay.

  • Royalties pass through after the stores pay
  • PLAIGROUND takes none
03

Who can withdraw

Basic can see totals but cannot withdraw. Upgrade to Creator or Pro. Creator and Pro can retrieve payouts.

  • Basic: see totals, no withdraw
  • Creator and Pro: retrieve payouts
