Create Music Group just bought a piece of DistroKid. The pipes are consolidating.

Oct 29, 2026. Fewer owners. Same truth on upload.

Create Music Group took a minority stake in DistroKid earlier this year. Create is based in LA and backed by Flexpoint Ford and Ares. CVC Capital Partners already holds the majority stake, which closed in Q3. Create CEO and co-founder Jonathan Strauss confirmed that investment to Music Business Worldwide on October 28. In a statement to MBW, he said: “Create is home to some of the world’s largest and most storied independent record labels and artists, and DistroKid is where countless artists begin their careers. We’re excited to partner with CVC as a minority investor in the company and to support its continued growth.”

That's a big deal for a small reason. DistroKid moves a huge share of the new music that comes out every day. When fewer companies own the pipes, they get more say over what gets through.

DistroKid is also still being sued by UMG over AI music. So the biggest pipe for new releases is now owned by investors and fighting a major label in court, all at the same time.

What does that mean for you? Probably nothing today. Your songs still go out. But the rules can change when the owners change, and AI music is where those rules are moving fastest.
