How to pick a distributor for AI-assisted music

What to check before you choose a distributor when AI helped make your songs.

Part 1

Not every distributor treats AI the same

Some services have tightened rules on AI uploads. Before you pick one, read their AI policy, not just their price.

Part 2

What to check

  • Do they accept AI-assisted music, and how do they label it?
  • Do they take a cut of your royalties?
  • Do they handle split sheets and co-writers?
  • Is the full price shown before you pay?

Part 3

How plai handles it

Every release is labeled Human, AI-assisted or Fully AI. Splits are signed before delivery. We take none of your recording royalties.
