Preview: the MMF-US LA Summit on Nov 9

Nov 3, 2026. The published agenda, the room, and where a manager's AI questions still sit.

By Victoria Imtanes, Editor

This is a preview of a summit that has not happened yet. Everything below is taken from the Music Managers Forum US page for the event. Where that page is silent, this post is silent too.

When and where

MMF-US has scheduled the LA Summit for Monday, November 9, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Doors and check-in begin at 9:00 AM. The location on the page is Spotify's Mateo Campus, 555 Mateo Street, B2, Los Angeles, CA 90013.

The page describes a full day of keynote conversations, panels, presentations, and networking for artist managers, self-managed artists, and industry professionals. It says attendees will also receive follow-up materials, tools, and resources after the event. It also says more speakers and panelists will be announced.

What the agenda actually lists

The summit page does not name a session about artificial intelligence. It does name these panels and presentations: Music Managers: The New Power Brokers, Know Your Rights, Independent Music Financing, Independent Distribution, Driving Social Change Through Music, and The Business Behind the Artist: Infrastructure and the Modern Team. It adds "and more."

The keynote is Marcie Allen, Co-Owner of Anzie Blue, in conversation with Jeanine McLean, CEO of Castlight Media & Management. MMF-US says Allen has more than 25 years in music and entertainment, that she founded MAC Presents, and that she is co-owner of Nashville's independent venue Anzie Blue. The page says her work has connected artists including the Rolling Stones, Katy Perry, John Legend, Keith Urban, Tim McGraw, and Faith Hill with brands. It says McLean's management career includes working with Alicia Keys and H.E.R., and that her current roster includes Omarion, Tone Stith, and Brittney Spencer. It says she has been recognized by Billboard, Pollstar, Variety, and Ebony. Those are the bios as published. This post does not add claims about what either of them will say.

Where AI questions sit, even so

A manager walking into those rooms in November 2026 is already living with AI, whether or not a panel is titled that way. The page does not say the panels will cover it. These are the questions those published topics leave in a manager's hands.

Know Your Rights is the room for ownership. If a tool helped write a melody, generate a vocal, or imitate a tone, someone still has to say who owns the recording and the song. A style is not the same thing as a specific track. The rights conversation is where that difference gets practical: what was made by a person, what was generated, and what still needs a yes from someone else.

Independent Distribution is the room for the path to stores. Stores and distributors are asking, in their own policies, whether a release is human, assisted, or generated. A manager who cannot answer that in one sentence is guessing on the way in.

Independent Music Financing is the room for whether the catalog can be borrowed against. An advance depends on what the music is and who controls it. If part of a catalog was generated, the manager needs a plain account of that before a funder does.

The Business Behind the Artist is the room for the team. Who writes, who performs, who edits, who pitches, and what a tool did instead. Infrastructure is a boring word for a real job: the credits have to name the humans, and the label has to match the file.

None of that is a prediction of the day. It is the reason those four themes matter to anyone managing an artist while the tools change. The social-change panel and the "power brokers" panel are on the same page. MMF-US has not published descriptions of them beyond the titles, so this preview stops at the titles.

Tickets, as listed

MMF-US lists general admission at $100. It says current members attend free, limited to one ticket per member, using a code from the member portal. It lists a new-member price of $50 with the code WELCOMETOMMFUSLA, and a renewing-member price of $50, normally $99, with the code WELCOMEBACKLA. Space is limited. Check the page before you pay. Prices and codes can change, and this post will not guess at a later version.
