Questions

Quick answers

What is an EPK? An EPK, or electronic press kit, is one link that shows bookers, press and playlist curators who you are: bio, music, photos, socials and contact.

What goes in an electronic press kit? A short honest bio, your best tracks, a few photos with one consistent look, a one-sheet with release facts, your socials, and how to reach you.

Does my EPK say if AI was used? Yes. Your EPK carries the same disclosure label as your releases: Human, AI-assisted, or Fully AI.

Do I need to hire someone to make one? No. You can fill in the template yourself. If you want help, the Persona package and Act Pack are paid options shown on this page.

Does an EPK guarantee bookings? No. A clear EPK makes you easier to book, but bookings depend on your music, your audience and the people you reach out to.
