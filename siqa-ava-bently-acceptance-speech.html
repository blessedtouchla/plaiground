This is just the beginning

Ava Bently's acceptance speech. SIQA Music Awards, Fri Nov 13, 2026. Announced on stage as Best AI Country Song. 4:19.

Ava Bently's full acceptance speech at the SIQA Music Awards, Fri Nov 13, 2026. It was announced on stage as the first AI Music Award, Best AI Country Song.

The video on this page is the full 4:19 speech, kept uncut. She thanks the two hosts. She says that one year ago she was fighting for her life with congestive heart failure, and that she wrote these songs as a legacy for her family. She calls the moment the beginning.
