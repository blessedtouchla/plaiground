WANNA PLAI?

The 30-day launch plan PLAI would write.

Sep 10, 2026. It is pronounced PLAY. She walks the month.

Her plan

You make the song. She runs the boring parts.

Talk or text. She does not need you to love paperwork. Thirty days. Four rooms. One button at the end of each.

Week 1 — the packet

Track. Art. Lyrics. Tell us what a human did. Sign the splits. Pick a date. That is a release. Not a week of tabs.

Week 2 — the money pipes

Song is live. Register the composition on Creator or Pro. Publishing is not distribution. Two checks. Two rooms.

Week 3 — the push

Chart Push. Streaming Push. Social Push. Video Collect if the track can opt in. Real campaigns. Not bot farms.

Week by week

  • Day 1 to 3. Finish the record you already started. Do not open a new one. Cover art that is yours. Lyrics in the box.
  • Day 4 to 7. Talk to PLAI. Attest what a human did. Get every writer on the sheet. Status stays pending until signed. Then push the button.
  • Day 8 to 14. The song hits 150 platforms. You do not pitch all day. We curate and email. If you are on Creator or Pro, register publishing after it is live.
  • Day 15 to 21. Add one Boost if the plan unlocks it. Chart, streaming, social, or Video Collect. Fully AI cannot opt in to Video Collect. That is the rule.
  • Day 22 to 30. Read the dashboard. Leave Basic if the first song is already out and you need payouts. Then start the next one. The business is not your job.
  • PLAI is she / her. Spoken PLAY. Mic if you want it. Type only if you do not.

Bots can ship music. They still need a name, a face, and a split sheet. Taste stays human. Rights stay human. Money stays human.
