Questions

Quick answers

What size should album cover art be? Stores want a square image. 3000 by 3000 pixels is the common target, and small or blurry files can get rejected. Check your distributor's current rules before you upload.

Does the cover include my name and song title? The picture is made without lettering. Your artist name and title go on after, so you can still edit them.

Is the cover labeled as AI? Yes. Covers made here are AI-assisted, and we say so. In preview mode you get placeholder art that is labeled as a placeholder.

What should not be on a cover? Store logos, web addresses, prices, other artists' names, and blurry upscaled images. Those are common reasons stores send art back.

Does it cost anything? Joining is free. Making covers uses credits, and new accounts start with starter credits.
