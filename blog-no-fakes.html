WANNA PLAI?

NO FAKES. No clones. No dead-singer covers.

Sep 16, 2026. The bright line. We will not ship that file.

The line

Original work is welcome. Stolen voices are not.

Traditional. AI-assisted. Fully AI. All three can live here. A clone of a living act cannot. A dead singer pulled back into a new hook cannot. That is not a grey area. That is a reject.

Allowed

Your voice. Your writing. Your model output that does not impersonate a real artist. A named persona with a human showrunner.

Not allowed

Voice clones of existing artists. Dead-singer covers. Lookalike names. Lookalike faces. Anything built to confuse a fan.

Who decides

You disclose. We review. PLAI asks the question on every upload. The packet has to match the file.

Why this is the product

  • Stores care. Charts care. Brands care. A fake voice is a takedown waiting on a clock.
  • EU transparency rules already treat audio that resembles a real person as a deepfake duty. Artistic use still needs a disclosure that does not hide.
  • Spotify is badging AI Personas. Apple is labeling tracks materially generated with AI. Luminate is building an AI Generated flag into the data that feeds charts.
  • SIVA is the example. A name. A face. A split sheet. Not a celebrity skin.
  • Taste stays human. Rights stay human. Money stays human. The bot does not get a free pass.

We still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay. Honesty is not the same thing as impersonation. One we ship. One we delete.

If you can buy a car at the click of a button, you should be able to release a song the same way. The car is not someone else’s.
