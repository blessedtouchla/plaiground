WANNA PLAI?

NO FAKES. No clones. No dead-singer covers.

Sep 16, 2026. The bright line. We will not ship that file.

The line

Original work is welcome. Stolen voices are not.

Traditional. AI-assisted. Fully AI. All three can live here. A clone of a living act cannot. A dead singer pulled back into a new hook cannot. That is not a grey area. That is a reject.

Allowed

Your voice. Your writing. Your model output that does not impersonate a real artist. A named persona with a human showrunner.

Not allowed

Voice clones of existing artists. Dead-singer covers. Lookalike names. Lookalike faces. Inaccurate disclosure. Anything built to confuse a fan.

Who decides

You disclose. We review. PLAI asks the question on every upload. The packet has to match the file.

Why this is the product

  • Stores care. Charts care. Brands care. A fake voice is a takedown waiting on a clock.
  • Every upload asks what a human did. No AI. AI-assisted. Fully AI. Lie on the field and the release is not clean.
  • SIVA is the example. A name. A face. A split sheet. Not a celebrity skin. Taste stays human. Rights stay human. Money stays human.
  • Video Collect: original audio only. Fully AI cannot opt in. Covers, samples, and already-claimed conflicts get a review.
  • We still deliver Fully AI even if a store will not pay. Honesty is not impersonation. One we ship. One we delete.
  • If the song needs someone else’s identity to work, it does not ship.

If I can buy a car at the click of a button, I should be able to upload my music like that too. The car is not someone else’s.
