Submitted, Registered, Matched, and Paid appear only when that receipt is on file.

Who can be registered

Distribution can accept a Fully AI recording. Work registration cannot. BMI does not register a work made only from prompts. BMI, ASCAP, and SOCAN accept a partially AI-generated work when a human wrote part of it and that part meets the U.S. Copyright Office human-creativity threshold. If the contribution is unclear, the request stays in review.

Sources: BMI and AI, BMI work registration terms, ASCAP, BMI, and SOCAN, 28 Oct 2025.
