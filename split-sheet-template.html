Make a split sheet

The template

What to fill in

  • Song title and date written.
  • Legal name, email and PRO for each writer, such as BMI or ASCAP.
  • IPI number for each writer, if they have one.
  • A share for each writer. All shares add to 100%.
  • A signature from every writer.

Questions

Quick answers

What goes on a split sheet template? The song title and date, the legal name, email and PRO for each writer, an IPI number if they have one, each share, and every signature. All shares add to 100%.

How do I fill one out on plai? Add writers and percents when you release. Writer 1 signs in the page, and co-writers get an email to sign. Delivery waits until everyone has signed.

Do the shares have to add up to 100%? Yes. Enter a share for each writer. The shares must add to 100% before anyone signs.

What if I am the only writer? List your legal name and 100%. If someone else wrote melody or lyrics, add that person and get a signature too.
