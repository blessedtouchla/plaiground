Fully Gen AI

Typed a prompt into a generator.

Downloaded the track.

Model made the composition, the instruments, and the vocals.

Partly Gen AI

AI made one layer. A bassline. Strings. A melody sketch.

You recorded your own vocals and instruments over it.

That is Partly Gen AI.

Same label if AI helped with lyric ideas, or a text-to-video model made the visuals on a 100% human audio track.

No Gen AI

Auto-Tune. Pitch correction.

AI mastering.

YouTube says those do not count.

They are production tools, not generation.
