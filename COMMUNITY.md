# Plai+ Community v1

AI SoundCloud slice. Not distribution. Do not touch store-client, ToneGrid hop, cover send, or splits.

## Doors
- Anyone can visit Community and listen.
- Sign up on Community without a wannaplai plan.
- Existing wannaplai login is the same person here.
- Community-only account can attach distribution later (same email / user id).
- No plan wall. Everyone signed in can post after they have a profile name.

## Names
- Nav label: Community
- Product line / badge: Plai+

## V1 ship
1. Community page + signup/login (reuse site auth if present).
2. Profile: name, photo, one-liner. NO disclosure quiz on profile.
3. Post a song: manual file upload only (no Suno import, no bulk).
4. Per-song disclosure (required) then feed.
5. Filter feed by badges later if time; v1 can show badges on the card.

Out of v1: DMs, algorithm, Connect Suno/Udio OAuth, AI avatar generator, embed player, iOS-native feed.

## Song disclosure

Step 1 required — one of:
- Full AI
- AI assisted
- No AI

Step 2 only if AI assisted — multi-select, required to earn human badges:
- Wrote the lyrics → HUMAN Words
- Sang / recorded vocals → HUMAN Vox
- Played an instrument → HUMAN Played
- Arranged / produced / mixed → HUMAN Mix

Assisted with zero checks still posts as AI assisted, no human badge.

Optional platform (hidden when No AI):
- Suno / Udio / Other / Prefer not to say

Badges live on the TRACK. Profile may later roll up counts. Never ask this at profile create.

## Rules
- One track per post in v1.
- Audio = user file (wav/mp3/flac).
- No ToneGrid send from Community posts.
- New files only for this work.
