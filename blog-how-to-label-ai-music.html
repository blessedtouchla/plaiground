Human, AI-assisted or Fully AI: how to label your music

A simple way to decide which label fits your song, and why it matters.

Part 1

Ask what a human did

Did a person write the lyrics, the melody, or perform the vocal? The more real creative choices a human made, the closer you are to Human.

Part 2

Pick the label

  • Human: people wrote and performed it.
  • AI-assisted: a human made key creative choices and AI helped.
  • Fully AI: generated with little human authorship.

Part 3

Why honesty wins

Stores are adding AI rules and listeners want to know. An honest label helps protect your catalog.
