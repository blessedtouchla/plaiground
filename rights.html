By submitting a release you confirm that you hold the rights to the recording, that you have disclosed AI-assisted portions honestly, and that no part of the work clones an existing artist’s voice or master.

Human authorship

Fully AI-generated tracks can still be distributed and monetized. They just cannot be published, because publishing registration needs a human author. At least one human contribution is required to claim authorship and collect writer royalties.

Publisher appointment

If you pay for publishing registration, PLAIGROUND LLC is appointed publisher of record for that work at 100% of the publisher’s half. Your writer share is set on the split sheet and is unaffected by that appointment.
