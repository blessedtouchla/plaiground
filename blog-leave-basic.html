When to leave Basic.

Sep 6, 2026. Free gets the first song out. Paid gets the money moving.

The free tier

Basic is not a trial. It is one song for life.

Upload. Split sheets. Live on 150 platforms. You see the royalty totals. You cannot withdraw them. No publishing. No Boost. No second release.

Stay on Basic

You only have one finished track. You are testing the flow. You do not need publishing or payouts yet.

Leave Basic

You need a second song. You want the publishing check. You want the money in your account. You want a Boost.

What unlocks

Creator: publishing, Boosts, analytics, retrieve / get paid, 8 distro + 8 publishing a month. Pro: unlimited + catalog migration.

The clear signals

  • You finished song two. Basic only holds one release for life. The next upload needs Creator or Pro.
  • Royalties are showing and you want them. Basic can display totals. Only Creator and Pro can withdraw.
  • You want the song registered for publishing. Mechanical and performance money sits on a different ledger. Publishing is Creator and Pro only.
  • You want a Chart Push, Streaming Push, Social Push, or Video Collect. Boosts unlock on Creator and Pro. They are not for sale on the public site.
  • You have an existing catalog to move. Catalog migration is Pro.
  • You are releasing more than eight tracks a month. Creator caps at eight distribution uploads and eight publishing registrations. Pro removes the caps.

Basic is the front door. Creator is the first paid floor. Pro is the unlimited floor.
