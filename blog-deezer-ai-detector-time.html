Deezer's AI detector made TIME's Best Inventions.

Oct 22, 2026. The robots are being counted.

An AI detector just won a trophy.

TIME named Deezer's AI music detection tool one of its Best Inventions of 2026.

The numbers Deezer shared

Nearly 90,000 fully AI tracks arrive at Deezer every day.

On peak days that is more than half of everything delivered.

Over 42 million AI tracks were pulled from its recommendations and editorial playlists between January 2025 and August 2026.

Deezer says the tool is 99.8% accurate.

Why they built it

Deezer says fully AI music is only 1 to 3% of streams.

But it found up to 85% of streams on fully AI tracks in 2025 were fraud. Those streams get no royalties.

Deezer tags AI-generated music so listeners can see it.
