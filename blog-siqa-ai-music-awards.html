Preview: the SIQA AI Music Awards at The Mayan

Nov 3, 2026. Nov 13 at The Mayan in Los Angeles. Only what SIQA's own pages list.

By Victoria Imtanes, Editor

SIQA, the Sonic Intelligence Academy, is holding its 2026 AI Music Awards over two days in Downtown Los Angeles. This preview is about the ceremony on Friday, November 13, 2026. The facts below come from SIQA's awards site. A few lines on that site do not match each other. Those mismatches are included, not smoothed over.

The night

SIQA's homepage says November 13 is the AIMAs at The Mayan in Downtown Los Angeles. The nominees page lists the venue as The Mayan, 1038 S Hill St, with carpet doors at 6:30 PM and a global livestream. SIQA describes the building as a 1927 landmark and describes the show as a red carpet plus live hybrid human and AI performances, with a live band and immersive visuals. The homepage says the ceremony is capped at 600 seats, with fewer than 100 released to the public.

The day before is separate. The homepage lists Thursday, November 12 as a SIQA Summit and Premiere Ceremony in Downtown Los Angeles: panels and keynotes, demo lounges, a closing fireside chat it says is "announcing soon," the first AIMA honors, and a networking mixer. It does not place that day at The Mayan. Summit tickets are listed at $99. Awards tickets for November 13 are listed at $250. VIP requests go to team@thesiqa.com. Those prices are what the site showed on Nov 3, 2026.

How many awards

Both pages say there are 25 award categories, and both print the same groups. The homepage's day-two card also says "13 Awards On Stage," building through the night to AI Song of the Year. The nominees page opens with a line about twenty awards on stage, also building to AI Song of the Year. This post does not know which on-stage count SIQA will use. The category list below is the one both pages print. It adds up to 25.

General field, 4: AI Song of the Year, AI Artist of the Year, AI Album of the Year, AI Producer of the Year.

Artist and performance, 3: Best AI Group, Best AI Music Video, Best AI Collab.

Classification songs, 2: Best AI-Assisted Song, Best Human+AI Hybrid Song.

Genre songs, 10: Best AI R&B Song, Best AI Country Song, Best AI Gospel Song, Best AI Pop Song, Best AI Rock/Alt Song, Best AI Hip-Hop Song, Best AI International Song, Best AI Comedic Song, Best AI Musical Theatre Song, Best AI Jazz Song.

Genre artists, 6: Best AI R&B Artist, Best AI Country Artist, Best AI Gospel Artist, Best AI Pop Artist, Best AI Rock/Alt Artist, Best AI Hip-Hop Artist.

After that list, SIQA describes the Off the Charts Spotlight Award. The site says it honors an artist who stood out in the Off the Charts Live AI Music Series, and that it is the one category decided by the SIQA team. Everything else, the site says, is decided by the artists themselves. The Spotlight is not given a number inside the five groups above.

Nominees we could read on the page

The nominees page says every nominee is a verified artist in the SIQA Registry, and that you pick a category to see who is in the running across all 25 awards. The board that loads with the page is AI Song of the Year. It says there are 5 nominees, decided by verified SIQA Registry artists. The five shown are:

  • Already Her, Nova Solé
  • Let 'Em, Waking Up Christopher
  • Morning Magic, Millionaire Muses
  • God Don't Play About Me, Aria Blu
  • How Was I Supposed To Know?, Xania Monet

Other category boards did not load in the page's initial HTML, so this preview does not list those nominees. Use the nominees page for the rest. We are not filling the gap from other sites.

The awards site's event data also names four performers: Xania Monet, Olivia B Moore, Chaisen Hale, and EJ Rogers. The visible performers section does not print that list. It says the night includes performances from some of the top-charting artists on the SIQA AI Music Charts. Treat the four names as what the event data says, not as a full bill.

Who votes

SIQA says the first AIMAs are decided by verified artists in the SIQA Registry. The page describes four steps: get verified through disclosure rather than detection, claim a profile so verified artists can vote, submit eligible work to the categories it belongs in, and vote a ballot. It says you cannot vote for your own work. It also says full methodology, eligibility windows, and ethics rules publish before voting opens. Those rules were not on the pages read for this preview.

Sources, read Nov 3, 2026: SIQA, The 2026 AI Music Awards and SIQA nominees. Related here: how to label AI music and SIQA Charts.
