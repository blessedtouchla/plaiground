Get paid

Register with SoundExchange as a featured artist

Sign up as a featured artist so SoundExchange can pay your share when your recordings play on digital radio.

Time: About 20 to 30 minutes, plus a few days for approval.

Have ready

What you need

  • Your legal name, date of birth and contact email
  • Your Social Security number or tax ID for tax forms
  • A government photo ID and a phone with a camera for the ID check
  • Your artist name exactly as it appears on your releases

Steps

How to do it

  1. Go to soundexchange.com and choose Register, then pick Performer (not Sound Recording Copyright Owner).
  2. Confirm you are 18 or older.
  3. Enter your date of birth, legal name, gender and contact details.
  4. Verify your photo ID. You pick your country, then take photos of your ID and a selfie on your phone.
  5. Add your artist or band name and how you want to be paid. Direct deposit pays faster than a paper check.
  6. Review and submit. Optional: sign the free membership with your typed legal name.

Good to know

What this does

SoundExchange collects for digital radio such as satellite radio, Pandora radio and webcasters. It helps you collect the featured artist share when those services play your recordings. Fully AI-generated recordings may not qualify. Recordings with real human creative input may. No signup guarantees income. You may earn when your music is played and reported.

Official source: www.soundexchange.com
